Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Goldline Brief (@GoldlineBrief) handles data in connection with its desktop video publishing utility and connected platform APIs.

1. Information Collected

This tool operates locally on the creator's workstation and stores only OAuth access and refresh tokens required to upload creator-owned videos to authorized YouTube and TikTok accounts. We do not collect, sell, or share personal data from public third-party users.

2. How Data Is Used

Authorized API tokens are used solely to upload video files, titles, descriptions, and hashtags initiated by the account owner via the Content Posting API.

3. Data Storage & Revocation

Tokens are stored locally in restricted files on the creator's device and may be revoked at any time from the corresponding platform account security settings.
